{# DRIVER-IMPACT TORNADO — screen version (Aurora design tokens) ============================================================== Horizontal bar chart that ranks the engine's calibration steps by €-impact on enterprise value, descending. The chart is the M&A reader's answer to "which adjustments dominate the headline number?". Data source: `multiple_pipeline.discount_waterfall` (preferred) or `multiple_pipeline.stages` (legacy fallback). Each step's € impact is computed as the basis (sustainable EBITDA) × the multiple delta the step contributed. We DO NOT re-compute discounts — the engine is the source of truth; we simply visualise what's already there. Why this complements the existing waterfall component: - The waterfall (`_multiple_waterfall_chart.html`) shows the *path* from base multiple to applied multiple. Chronological narrative. - This tornado shows the *magnitude ranking*. Decision-quality view — a CFO asks "what moves the number most?", not "in what order did the engine apply discounts?". Both surfaces use the same underlying data; the divergent geometry reflects the divergent question. Defensive: silently hidden when there are fewer than 2 non-trivial steps (a single-step "ranking" is meaningless), and when the EBITDA basis is unavailable (the €-impact axis would be unscaled).
